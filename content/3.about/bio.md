description: >-
  Maria Clara is an artist and set designer based between Geneva and Lugano. \

  In her research and practice, she explores space as a dramaturgical driving force, a site of tension between the intentional and the accidental. She focuses on how this dialogue generates unexpected spatial narratives, both in reality as well as in fiction. \

  She graduated in Art and Theatre History (Alma Mater Studiorum, Bologna), in Interior Architecture (Head, Genève) and in Scenography (La Manufacture, Lausanne). As a scenographer, she collaborated with Mathilde Monnier, Virgile Dagneaux, and Daniele Pintaudi. She worked as teaching assistant in Head – Genève. 

  She is an artist-in-residence at the Comédie de Genève for the 2024–2027 period, in collaboration with director Eléonore Bonah. She is co-author of the play *Lenz* (2024). Together with Bonah, she is currently working on a new production (*Chrüsimüsi*, 2026).
