title: Bio
description: >-
  Maria Clara is an artist and scenographer based between Geneva and Lugano.\

  In her research and practice, she explores space as a dramaturgical driving force and as a site of tension between the intentional and the accidental. She focuses on how this dialogue generates unexpected spatial narratives, both in reality and in fiction, drawing inspiration from the theatrical potential of urban spaces. She investigated these possibilities through *Notti Future* (2024), *Curtain Up!* (2025), and *Ti sei seduta dietro una vetrina* (2026).\

  She is an artist-in-residence at the Comédie de Genève for the 2024–2027 period, in collaboration with director Eléonore Bonah. Together, they wrote and directed the plays *Chrüsimüsi* (2026) and *Lenz* (2024).\

  She holds degrees in Art and Theatre History from Alma Mater Studiorum – University of Bologna, Interior Architecture from HEAD – Genève, and Scenography from La Manufacture, Lausanne.\

  As a set designer, she has collaborated with Mathilde Monnier, Virgile Dagneaux, and Daniele Pintaudi. She has also worked as a teaching assistant at HEAD – Genève.
