description: >-
  *Chrüsimüsi* is a play that draws on the singular writing of Swiss author
  Adelheid Duvanel.\

  Through a series of short narratives, the work gives body and voice to a gallery of hypersensitive, marginal and solitary characters, whose inner worlds oscillate between the familiar and the uncanny.\

  The scenography explores the tension between the concrete and the imaginary, between everyday objects and dreamlike landscapes, where fragments of stories and gestures emerge and dissolve.\

  Performed in French, German and Italian, *Chrüsimüsi* embraces multilingualism as a dramaturgical material, where the difficulties of communication—deeply rooted in Duvanel’s universe—become a fertile ground for theatrical invention.
